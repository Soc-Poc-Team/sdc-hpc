# SDC Admin documentation

Welcome to the admin documentation of the `small-data-cluster`. First we would
like to thank you for checking out our project and inform you, that the
`small-data-cluster` is still a reserch product with very limited support by us.

## What does the SDC offer to you and your company

Manay companies in germany dont use the data that they would have at hand to
optimize their processes or learn more about their products, customers,
procedures. The SDC offers you a "On-demand" HPC on hardware you already own to
host a simple but powerfull platform for small HPC workloads like data analysis.
